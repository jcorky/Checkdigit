Container Check Digit Calculator

Calculate a missing digit or check a complete container number.

Enter 10 characters to calculate the missing check digit, or 11 to check a complete ISO 6346 number. Lower case, spaces and hyphens are accepted.

Other identifier types

ILU (EN 13044). Intermodal loading units use category letters A, B, D, E or K and the same mod-11 arithmetic as ISO 6346. The equivalence to the normative EN 13044-1 text is not independently confirmed here.

UIC wagon. Rail vehicle numbers are 11 digits plus a Luhn check digit (12 in total). Enter 11 digits to calculate the check digit, or 12 to check a complete number.

What this check covers

Checkdigit confirms two things: the number has a valid shape, and its printed check digit agrees with the body under the identifier's own arithmetic.

It does not confirm that a container exists or is registered, who owns it, where it is, or whether it may be released. A correct check digit also cannot catch every typo — two different bodies can share a check digit.
