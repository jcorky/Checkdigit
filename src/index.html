What it does

Four steps, nothing guessed

Each identifier is validated against the standard it actually uses. When the type is ambiguous, or the number came from somewhere that does not declare it as an equipment ID, CHECKDIGIT flags it instead of silently rewriting it.

01

Detect

Finds every equipment number by its shape: four letters and seven digits for containers, twelve digits for rail wagons.

02

Route

Sends each number to the right algorithm: ISO 6346 mod-11 for containers and ILU codes, Luhn mod-10 for UIC wagons. Never the wrong one.

03

Correct

Recomputes the check digit and replaces only that one character. Every other character is left exactly as it was.

04

Review

Shows what changed, what was flagged for a human, and why. Nothing is stored: close the tab and it is gone.

Coverage

Identifiers it knows

The arithmetic is the same one printed in the standards, and the site's calculator shows every step of it.

Identifier types

  • ISO 6346Maritime containers (category U, J or Z). Mod-11 check digit, recomputed and corrected.
  • ILU · EN 13044Intermodal loading units (category A, B, D, E or K). Same arithmetic, routed to its own category set.
  • UIC wagonTwelve-digit rail vehicle numbers. Luhn mod-10, never cross-applied.
  • Size / typeCodes such as 22G1 are descriptive, not check-digit targets. Never altered.

Verdicts

  • ValidThe check digit is correct.
  • CorrectedThe digit you typed is wrong; the correct digit is shown.
  • FlaggedSomething needs a human: an unknown category letter, a non-standard owner prefix, or a number found in free text.
  • InvalidThe text does not have the shape of any known identifier.

Flagged, not guessed. When a number fails its check but the context does not justify a silent fix, CHECKDIGIT flags it for review and shows the digit it would have used.

On your terms

In your browser, and quiet about your data

There is no account, no upload and no server. The pages are static files; the work happens in this tab.

Runs in your browser

No sign-up and no third-party upload. What you type or drop here never leaves this tab.

Stores nothing

There is no database, no log and no analytics. The site cannot remember a number because it has nowhere to put one.

No outside requests

No fonts, scripts or trackers from other hosts. After the first visit the pages load with no network at all.

Byte-exact correction

A corrected number differs from the original only at the check digit. Everything else is preserved.
